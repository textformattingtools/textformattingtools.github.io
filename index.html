About Text Formatting Tools

Text Formatting Tools is a fast, free, and lightweight browser utility inspired by the clean, minimal aesthetic of ConvertCase.net. Designed for writers, editors, programmers, and students, it lets you reformat messy text, adjust capitalization styles, alphabetize lists, clean up unformatted copy-pasted content, and analyze character and word statistics.

Unlike many online converters loaded with intrusive ads, tracking scripts, and slow server requests, our engine processes 100% of your data client-side within your browser. Your confidential emails, draft manuscripts, passwords, and source code never leave your computer.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action Description
Ctrl + Z / Cmd + Z Undo Revert to the previous text formatting step.

Available Specialized Tools

Looking for a dedicated workflow? Jump directly to our specialized tool pages:

  • Case Converter: Dedicated converter for UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, and more with full styling guides.

  • Sort Lines: Alphabetical A-Z, reverse Z-A, natural numeric sorting, duplicate remover, and line numbering.
