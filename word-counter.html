Understanding Text Metrics & Word Counts

Tracking text statistics is essential for social media character constraints (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram captions), academic thesis requirements, essays, blog post SEO optimization, and speech pacing.

Platform / Use Case Standard Limit / Target Recommended Reading Metric
X (Twitter) 280 characters Total characters including spaces.
Google SEO Meta Description 150 – 160 characters Total characters (descriptions over 160 characters get truncated in SERP).
Blog Post Articles 1,200 – 2,500 words 6 to 12 minutes estimated reading time (based on 200 WPM).
Keynote Speech / Podcast 130 words per minute Speaking time calculation (approx. 650 words per 5-minute talk).

Reading Speed vs. Speaking Pace

An average adult reads silently at a speed of approximately 200 to 250 words per minute (WPM). When speaking aloud in presentations, lectures, or broadcast media, natural speech slows down to approximately 130 to 150 words per minute to allow for clear pronunciation, audience comprehension, and pauses.
